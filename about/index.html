About

Parameswaran V Vaidyanathan

Parameswaran is a Principal Architect at AWS, where he works with customers architecting and operating mission-critical, multi-region resilient workloads. His work spans resilience engineering, chaos engineering, and building centralized platform tooling reused across engineering teams. He recently delivered a multi-region resilience and failover-orchestration session at Beer City Code 2026, including live failover demonstrations. His current focus is extending proven disaster-recovery patterns with sustainability signals — making the same infrastructure that keeps critical services running also responsive to grid carbon intensity.
